## Current phase

**Foundation / early scripture.**

Do not accelerate into a large manuscript yet.

Build one doctrine or short text at a time, test it against canon, then decide whether to promote it.

## Locked premise

Cthulhu is not the supreme god.

Cthulhu is **the First Witness**: the first known being to pass beyond the limits of ordinary finite perception and return with enough individuality to communicate.

He originally sought what lay beyond.

After experiencing it, he concluded that unrestricted exposure is not a gift.

## Locked theological decisions

1. **Cthulhu does not want worshippers.**
2. **Cthulhu does not want universal awakening.**
3. **The Veil is both limitation and protection.**
4. **The Great Sleep is voluntary containment, not merely imprisonment.**
5. **R'lyeh is sanctuary, threshold, monastery, and containment.**
6. **The Dream filters what lies beyond into forms finite minds may survive.**
7. **The Flood is the indirect movement of those filtered fragments through consciousness.**
8. **Nyarlathotep represents revelation without mercy or preparation.**
9. **Yog-Sothoth remains fundamentally unresolved.**
10. **Azathoth remains near the point where language fails.**
11. **Death and identity remain unsettled; no simple heaven/hell or immortality substitute.**
12. **The highest movement is not permanent escape from finite life, but return to it.**
13. **Cthulhu remains alien even when acting with restraint or compassion.**
14. **The scripture itself must undermine attachment to its own concepts.**
15. **Real religious traditions may inform structure and philosophical method, but are not recast as secret Cthulhu worship.**

## Locked writing decision

The sanity/insanity inversion is **subtext**.

Do not make words such as "sanity," "insanity," "sane," "madness," or "hyper-sanity" regular theological vocabulary.

The reader should infer the inversion.

Use imagery and consequences instead:

- seeing / not seeing
- waking / dreaming
- Veil
- Flood
- burden
- return
- drowning
- distance
- silence
- impossible memory
- small ordinary life after revelation

## Preferred central formulations

> **Cthulhu woke so that we would not have to.**

> **Do not worship the one who saw.**

> **What reaches you has already been made survivable.**

> **Glimpse the abyss. Then go home.**

These are guideposts, not lines that should be repeated mechanically throughout the manuscript.

## Canon workflow

For each new idea:

1. Check this file.
2. Check `CANON_RULES.md`.
3. Check existing YAML records for equivalent doctrine.
4. Decide whether the idea is canonical, accepted, disputed, apocryphal, or draft.
5. Add or update structured canon first.
6. Write prose afterward.
7. Check the prose against `SCRIPTURE_STYLE.md`.
8. Promote only after contradiction review.

## Drift alarms

Stop and review before continuing if new material does any of the following:

- makes Cthulhu a conventional god;
- makes worship desirable;
- treats awakening as automatically good;
- explains what lies beyond too neatly;
- overuses explicit sanity/insanity terminology;
- turns the theology into simple nihilism;
- makes Nyarlathotep merely a liar or devil analogue;
- resolves Yog-Sothoth or Azathoth too cleanly;
- loses the importance of returning to ordinary finite life;
- borrows directly from a real sacred text without source and rights review;
- contradicts an existing canonical record without deliberately marking the disagreement.
