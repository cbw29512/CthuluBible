# Scripture Style Guide

Read this before writing or revising canonical scripture.

## Core distinction

The **canon files may explain the theology directly**.

The **scripture should almost never explain it directly**.

The YAML knows the theology. The scripture experiences it.

## Primary rule

Do not repeatedly name the underlying sanity/insanity inversion.

The reader should infer it from events, images, dialogue, paradox, and consequences.

Avoid making words such as these routine doctrinal vocabulary:

- sanity
- insanity
- sane
- insane
- madness
- hyper-sanity

These words may appear rarely when a character naturally uses them, but they should not carry the theology.

## Preferred scriptural language

Favor indirect language such as:

- the Veil
- the Dream
- the Flood
- the Seeing
- the First Witness
- the Sleeper
- what lies beyond
- what was seen
- the place without distance
- that which remains
- what the eye was not shaped to hold
- the burden of sight
- the return
- bearing the sight

## Show before explaining

Do not write:

> Time is nonlinear beyond the Veil.

Prefer a story in which a disciple remembers a funeral that has not yet happened.

Do not write:

> The self is an illusion.

Prefer a dialogue in which a seeker cannot answer which version of the self they want preserved.

Do not write:

> Direct revelation destroys finite minds.

Prefer a scholar who completes an impossible proof, quietly erases the last line, and never attempts it again.

## Cthulhu's voice

Cthulhu is:

- restrained
- alien
- precise when precision helps
- silent when explanation would do harm
- unwilling to become an object of worship
- unwilling to claim complete understanding

He should not sound like a conventional benevolent deity.

He should often answer a question by exposing the assumption hidden inside it.

## Recursive method

A recurring teaching pattern:

1. Give the seeker a useful concept.
2. Let the seeker become attached to it.
3. Show the concept is incomplete.
4. Remove the seeker's certainty without removing meaning.
5. Return the seeker to ordinary life.

The text should repeatedly undermine attachment to its own explanations.

## Ordinary life matters

The return is essential.

After vast or terrifying revelation, use small finite details:

- insects at night
- cold hands
- bread
- salt
- a sleeping companion
- smoke from a small fire
- rain on a roof
- a familiar name
- grief
- laughter
- hunger

The point is not that ordinary life is secretly ultimate.

The point is that finite life remains worth inhabiting.

## Prohibitions

Canonical scripture should not:

- portray Cthulhu as seeking worship;
- make awakening everyone the goal;
- describe True Reality exhaustively;
- treat every vision or dream as genuine revelation;
- equate real-world mental illness with supernatural insight;
- turn real religions into secret Cthulhu cults;
- settle Yog-Sothoth or Azathoth into simple definitions;
- make Cthulhu psychologically human;
- explain every symbol after using it.

## Final test

Before promoting a passage from draft to scripture, ask:

> Could the reader discover the doctrine from this passage without the narrator announcing it?

If yes, the passage is probably moving in the right direction.
