# The Last Correct Answer

A student spent many years learning what the teachers had refused to make simple.

He learned that the Veil was not merely false.

He learned that the Dream concealed in order to reveal.

He learned not to bow toward the sea.

He learned to distrust every answer that made the vastness resemble a human face.

At last he came to his teacher carrying no icon, no prayer, and no request for awakening.

“I think I understand.”

The teacher nodded.

The student waited for correction.

None came.

So he continued.

“The Veil is necessary.”

The teacher nodded.

“The Sleeper is not a god.”

Again the teacher nodded.

“The Dream is translation.”

Again.

“The highest movement is return.”

Again.

The student felt a quiet satisfaction.

That night he dreamed of a room containing four stones.

Upon the first was written **VEIL**.

Upon the second, **SLEEPER**.

Upon the third, **DREAM**.

Upon the fourth, **RETURN**.

He recognized each one and knew why it was there.

Then the floor vanished.

The stones did not fall.

There was no direction in which they could fall.

The student reached for the stone marked **RETURN**.

His hand closed around his blanket.

Morning had come.

He went immediately to his teacher.

“I was wrong.”

“About which teaching?”

The student opened his mouth.

Then he understood the question.

He went outside instead.

A storm had scattered branches across the road.

He spent the morning clearing them with the neighbors.

At noon someone asked what his teacher had finally taught him.

The student looked back toward the house.

“I am not sure.”

The neighbor handed him another branch.

“Good. Take the heavy end.”

So he did.
