# The Answer Without Words

The keeper spoke the name a second time.

This time the Witness did not try to answer it.

The first calling had offered a shore.

The second offered another.

Between them there could be an interval.

And where there was an interval, something could be before and something after, though neither word was yet trustworthy.

The Witness held to neither calling as truth.

He held to the difference between them.

A difference permitted relation.

Relation permitted direction.

Not the old direction of stair and sea, but the smallest possible turning: from what could not be separated toward what had called twice.

On the stair, the keeper felt the clay cup move beneath his hand.

Only the width of a grain of sand.

He almost called again.

But the instruction had been two.

So he did not.

He sat beside the carved name until his legs ached.

Below him, the water touched the lowest step.

The Witness found the unanswered silence more useful than another voice.

The two callings remained distinct because there was no third to blur them into a chant.

Between them he began, imperfectly, to remember sequence.

With sequence came the possibility that one condition might follow another.

With that possibility came the first shape of return.

Not a road.

Not yet a self.

Only the knowledge that change need not mean disappearance.

Above, the keeper smelled the bread he had left beneath the cloth.

He was hungry.

Still he waited.
