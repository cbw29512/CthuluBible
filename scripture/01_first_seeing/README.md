# 01 — The Book of First Seeing

In progress.

Scope: Cthulhu's original crossing of the Veil and encounter with True Reality, followed by the difficult return to finite relation. The book establishes the First Witness and develops the first lived reasons for restraint, mediated contact, and eventual voluntary withdrawal without pretending to explain True Reality completely.

Current movement: seeking → crossing → return → recovered measure → chosen limitation → dangerous proximity → protective distance → mediated relationship → refusal of false certainty → revisable symbols → grief without metaphysical closure → identity distinguished from its tokens → involuntary leakage → chosen removal without abandonment → guidance measured by return → presence no longer requiring a signal → finite judgment without the Witness's guarantee → relationship without obligation or summons → gratitude redirected from the Witness toward finite life → attempted veneration refused without condemnation → the sacred place returned to ordinary use → refusal itself becoming ritual → accessibility recognized as a source of fixation → first contemplation of withdrawal beyond the paths made by attention.

The book should lead toward the Great Sleep without beginning it prematurely.

## Transmission convention

Before the First Seeing, Cthulhu may still be represented as using ordinary
speech and finite sequence.

After the First Seeing, quoted dialogue attributed to the Witness is no longer
to be read as a literal transcript of sounds made by a mouth. The post-Seeing
Witness communicates through impressions, juxtapositions, images, relations,
dream-forms, gestures, and survivable fragments of a mode humans cannot
faithfully hear or pronounce.

The keeper and later transmitters render those contacts into human sentences
so the text can be read. Different renderings may therefore disagree in wording
without requiring the underlying contact to have differed.

When a passage says "the Witness said," the canonical meaning is often closer
to: **this is how a finite mind later put the contact into words.**
