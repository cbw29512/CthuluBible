# 03 — The Book of the Veil

In progress.

Scope: wisdom and philosophical material exploring the Veil through the canon's recursive method: introduce a useful concept, then dismantle attachment to that concept without collapsing into meaninglessness.

Current movement: useful perception without absolute trust; mediation as measure rather than failure; identity examined without reducing a person to body, name, memory, object, or metaphysical promise; and return to ordinary obligation without requiring certainty.

The book should resist two symmetrical errors: treating finite categories as ultimate, and treating their incompleteness as proof that nothing matters.
