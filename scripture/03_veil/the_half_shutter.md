# The Half Shutter

A student covered every window in his house.

When his teacher asked why, the student said:

“I have learned that what I see is not what is.”

The teacher removed one board.

Light entered.

The student covered the opening with his hands.

“Do not restore the deception.”

The teacher asked him to bring water.

The student crossed the dark room, struck the table, and broke the pitcher.

He sat among the pieces.

“The wall protected me from false appearances,” he said.

The teacher answered:

“The wall protected you from appearances.”

The student was angry.

“Then should I trust the window?”

The teacher looked through it.

Outside, rain had begun.

“No.”

“Then why uncover it?”

The teacher placed the broken pieces of the pitcher on the sill.

“So that you may see enough to mend what you broke.”

The student watched the rain.

After a time he asked:

“What should I put between myself and what is?”

The teacher closed the shutter halfway.

“Enough.”
