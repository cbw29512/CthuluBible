# The Kindness Without Witness

A student came to the teacher after many nights of study.

“If what lies beyond us has no human face,” he said, “then perhaps kindness is only another picture we have painted upon it.”

The teacher was tying his sandals.

“Perhaps.”

The student followed him into the road.

“If the stars do not approve of kindness, why should I?”

The teacher did not answer.

Beyond the last house they found an old man beside an overturned cart.

One wheel had broken in the ditch.

The teacher lifted the axle while the student fitted stones beneath it.

They worked until their hands were muddy.

When the cart stood again, the old man thanked them and continued toward the village.

The student washed his hands in a stream.

“You did not answer me.”

The teacher sat on the bank.

“Did the stars help with the cart?”

“No.”

“Did the deep places command us?”

“No.”

“Was the man's burden lighter?”

The student looked back along the road.

“Yes.”

The teacher put on his sandals.

The student said, “Then kindness is true?”

The teacher shook his head.

“You are trying to put the cart back in the ditch.”

They walked home.
